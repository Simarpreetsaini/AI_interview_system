# 🤖 Advanced AI Interview System
## The Future of Objective, Scalable, and Smart Recruitment

**Mock Presentation**
*Speaker:* Simarpreet Singh

---

# 1. Introduction
**What is the Advanced AI Interview System?**

- A cutting-edge web platform that conducts automated, interactive interviews.
- It acts exactly like a human HR professional.
- It talks, listens, and watches the candidate in real-time.
- Fully powered by **Google Gemini AI** and **Computer Vision (FaceAPI)**.

---

# 2. The Need (Problem Statement)
**Why do we need this system?**

Traditional hiring processes face several major problems:
- ⏳ **Time Consuming:** HR teams spend hundreds of hours scheduling and conducting initial screening rounds.
- ⚖️ **Human Bias:** Interviewers can be biased based on mood, background, or fatigue.
- 📈 **Scalability Limits:** A company can only interview a limited number of people per day.
- ❌ **Inconsistent Questions:** Different candidates get different difficulty levels.

---

# 3. Our Solution
**How does our project solve this?**

- **24/7 Availability:** Candidates can take the interview anytime, anywhere.
- **100% Objective Scoring:** The AI scores everyone purely on their answers and technical accuracy.
- **Infinite Scalability:** We can interview 1,000 candidates at the exact same time.
- **Deep Analytics:** We provide the recruiter with a complete psychological and technical dossier (score, video, emotions).

---

# 4. Core Technologies
**What is under the hood?**

- **Backend Logic:** Python & FastAPI (Fast, modern, and highly scalable).
- **AI Brain:** Google Gemini 1.5 Flash (For real-time intelligent conversations).
- **Frontend / UI:** HTML5, CSS3 (Glassmorphism design), and Vanilla JavaScript.
- **Computer Vision:** FaceAPI.js (For tracking emotions and candidate presence).
- **Database:** SQLite / PostgreSQL (For storing secure records).

---

# 5. Key Features
**What makes this system unique?**

- 🎙️ **Two-Way Voice Communication:** It speaks to candidates and listens to their verbal answers.
- 📄 **Smart Resume Parsing:** Automatically extracts skills from uploaded CVs.
- 🧠 **Dynamic Questioning:** Questions are uniquely generated for every candidate based on their specific skills.
- 🛡️ **Anti-Cheat Integrity:** Detects if the candidate looks away, multiple faces appear, or they switch tabs.

# 9. The AI Brain: How it Thinks
**Behind the scenes of the Interview**

- **No two interviews are the same.** 
- The system takes the candidate's skills and injects a random seed into the Google Gemini AI.
- It forces the AI to ask **highly creative, scenario-based questions**.
- Example: Instead of asking *"What is Python?"*, it asks *"How would you debug a memory leak in a large Python web scraper?"*

---

# 10. Real-Time Emotion Tracking
**Reading the Candidate's State of Mind**

- As the candidate speaks, the webcam analyzes their facial expressions 10 times a second.
- It detects emotions: **Happy, Nervous, Neutral, Surprised**.
- This data is compiled into a "Primary Sentiment" score.
- This helps HR understand how the candidate handles pressure!

---

# 11. Security & Anti-Cheat
**Maintaining Interview Integrity**

Our system actively monitors for cheating:
- 🚫 **Tab Switching:** Warning if the candidate opens Google.
- 👤 **Face Missing:** Warning if the candidate walks away.
- 👥 **Multiple Faces:** Warning if someone else enters the frame to help.
- 🔊 **Background Noise:** Detects if someone is whispering answers.
Violations are logged in the final report!
